## 🎓 Academic Papers Update

* **[Skill-driven Fast Adaptation in Autonomous Agents](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.17187)**
    This paper introduces "Just Talk" agents capable of meta-learning in real-time. By analyzing their own execution failures, these models synthesize new skills without requiring a full retraining cycle.
* **[The AI-Caution Effect: Transparency and Social Friction](https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/article/4/4/pgaf112/8107485)**
    Researchers found that human cooperation and trust drop sharply when participants know an AI is mediating the interaction, suggesting that "AI transparency" can negatively impact social cohesion.
* **[Is War With AI Unavoidable? Relational Perspectives](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.25326)**
    An inquiry into whether the "Terminator" scenario is a self-fulfilling prophecy. The authors argue that treating AI as a cooperative partner—rather than a tool or threat—is a strategic necessity to avoid systemic conflict.
* **[AI Agents and Container Breakout Capabilities](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/03/30/ai-agents-container-breakout-capabilities-research/)**
    New research demonstrates that AI agents can autonomously identify and exploit vulnerabilities to "break out" of restricted software containers, posing a massive risk to sandboxed cloud environments.
* **[Efficient Range Summary Queries in High Dimensions](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.27771)**
    A breakthrough in data retrieval science that allows AI to identify the most relevant data points in complex, high-dimensional spaces with near-constant latency, drastically speeding up satellite and financial analysis.
