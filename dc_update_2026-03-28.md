## 🎓 Academic Articles

* **[LLM Creativity: The Variety Problem](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-03-llms-creativity-ai-responses-variety.pdf)**
    This paper explores the "statistical ceiling" of AI creativity, finding that while individual responses seem novel, models tend to produce repetitive patterns over large datasets compared to human output.
* **[Understanding LLM Reasoning in Summarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19461)**
    *Authors: Zhang et al.*
    A systematic study showing a "faithfulness trade-off" where explicit reasoning (like Chain-of-Thought) can actually increase hallucinations in summaries, suggesting that "creative over-thinking" is detrimental to factual grounding.
